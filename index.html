About Ignito Corporation

Ignito Corporation is a digitally driven startup founded in 2025 with a mission to bring smart, scalable, and affordable tech solutions to local businesses across India. Officially registered as a Sole Proprietorship under the MSME initiative of the Government of India, Ignito Corporation bears the MSME Registration Number: UDYAM-MP-37-0051379.

Our flagship project — the Smart LPG Distribution App — revolutionizes how gas distributors operate by enabling digital tracking, automated delivery records, analytics, reporting, workforce management, and real-time performance dashboards. Built in just one month by an 8-member team in remote collaboration, the app stands as a testament to our execution capabilities and innovation-first approach.

From startups to enterprises, we offer end-to-end services including Website Development, Mobile App Development, UI/UX Design, Backend/API setup, Hosting, SEO, Deployment & Maintenance — all under one digital roof. Our solutions are cost-effective, responsive, and tailored to client needs.

As we continue to scale, Ignito remains committed to solving real-world problems with smart technology — empowering the next generation of businesses through innovation, collaboration, and digital transformation.

Project

Smart LPG Distribution App is Ignito Corporation’s flagship project. It is designed to streamline LPG delivery, tracking, stock management, and worker-distributor coordination.

Built completely in remote mode within just one month, this project showcases the power of digital teamwork and innovation. An 8-member team worked collaboratively under expert mentorship to deliver a fully functional, scalable, and modern solution tailored specifically for the LPG industry.

With robust role-based access, automated delivery tracking, and performance dashboards, the app empowers distributors, managers, and workers to manage operations smartly across India — all in one unified platform.

Project Roadmap

Phase 1

Ideation and Research - Understanding the LPG distribution system and identifying gaps.

Phase 2

App Design & Development - Creating the Smart LPG Distribution App with roles.

Phase 3

Testing and Internal Team Trials - Ensuring tracking accuracy and efficiency.

Phase 4

Subscription Integration & Deployment - Monetization via Razorpay.

Phase 5

Team Expansion & Market Testing - Enhancing with feedback and scaling features.

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Smart Distributor App ("we", "our", or "the App") developed and operated by Ignito Corporation, a sole proprietorship owned by Poonam Kale, collects, uses, discloses, and protects the personal data of users. By using the App, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information from app users:

  • Personal Information:
    • Name, contact number, and address of customers and hawkers
    • Manager, worker, and delivery personnel details
    • Agency/distributor business name and distributor code
  • Transactional Information:
    • Cylinder stock in/out details
    • Delivery records and payment updates
    • Customer connection and disconnection logs
  • Device Information:
    • Device type, operating system, and usage analytics (for performance improvement)

Note: We do not access, collect, or store users' real-time or background location data. The Smart Distributor App functions without requiring any GPS or geolocation permissions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To manage LPG stock and deliveries efficiently
  • To provide a personalized dashboard and user experience
  • To maintain records of users, hawkers, customers, and workers
  • To generate delivery and sales reports
  • To enhance the performance and security of the app
  • To send relevant updates or service-related communication

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Your information is only shared:

  • With team members added by the account owner (e.g., manager, worker, hawker)
  • When required by law or regulatory authorities
  • With cloud service providers for secure data storage and backup

5. Data Security

We implement standard security measures including encrypted cloud storage and authentication to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or deletion.

6. User Control and Responsibility

  • Only the distributor (account owner) can manage roles and access
  • It is the responsibility of the user to keep login credentials confidential
  • Users can request deletion of their data by contacting us

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users will be notified of significant changes through the app or via registered email.
